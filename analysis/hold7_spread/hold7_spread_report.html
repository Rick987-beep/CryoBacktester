Distance = |short strike − long strike|. For short puts the long is below the short; for short calls above. The distance scales roughly with BTC spot price: the short is selected at ~10–12% OTM (Δ≈0.15), and the long at ~15–18% OTM (Δ≈0.05), so the gap is typically 5–8% of the current BTC price. At $85k BTC that is $4–7k; at $115k BTC it is $6–9k.

Why the distances look large in dollar terms: BTC options are quoted at strikes in $1,000 increments (sometimes $2,000+ at the wings). The nearest available strike to Δ=0.05 can be 1–3 strikes away from the theoretical value, causing the actual long delta to range from 0.03 to 0.10 even when targeting 0.05. In low-spot environments (BTC ~$65–70k) the $60k Deribit strike floor is sometimes the only option below the short, pushing the actual long delta higher than intended (e.g. Δ≈0.10 instead of 0.05).

Fee Model Verification

Fees are applied to both the long entry and long exit using the same model as the backtester (backtester/pricing.py · deribit_fee_per_leg):

ParameterValueFormula
Index rate0.025%fee_base = 0.00025 × BTC_spot per contract
Price cap fraction10.42%fee_cap = 0.1042 × option_price_usd
Applied feemin of abovemin(fee_base, fee_cap) per leg per trade
Worthless exit$0No fee charged when bid = 0 (option expires worthless)

Bug fixed in this run: An earlier draft used 0.03% / 12.5% (Deribit taker headline rates). The backtester config uses 0.025% / 10.42%, consistent with maker-equivalent fill assumptions. The corrected fees reduced the overstated hedge cost by ~$3–5 per leg, improving spread P&L by roughly $800–1,500 across the dataset depending on config. Fee functions were cross-validated against deribit_fee_per_leg() for six representative (spot, price) pairs — all matched to 4 decimal places.

Interpretation Notes

  • The protective long expires worthless in the vast majority of trades — the entire ask premium is lost. This is the expected cost of an OTM insurance leg.
  • Deep OTM options have wide bid/ask spreads (median ~9% of mark at Δ=0.15; worse for Δ<0.08). The ask-entry fill is the conservative, realistic assumption.
  • At lower delta targets (Δ=0.03), the option is very cheap but rarely has material value at exit — hedge is near-theoretical.
  • On stop-loss events the long leg gains from delta and vega expansion. However, the 2 SL events in this window were IV-spike stops (spot barely moved), so the far-OTM long gained very little against a short that was still far OTM itself.
  • The 106 excluded early trades (Oct 2024 – Apr 2025) had similar short P&L characteristics based on the full-period report (~+$37k). Their exclusion doesn't change the conclusion about relative spread cost.
