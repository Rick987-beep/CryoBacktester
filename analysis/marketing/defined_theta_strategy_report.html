01 / Executive Summary

A Disciplined Machine for Harvesting Volatility Premium

Defined Theta sells short-dated listed Bitcoin options and holds them into the next daily expiry. Entry is selective: the book reads implied volatility against subsequent realised vol, and it reads listed skew, then opens only when that combination looks worth writing. Every short is paired at open with an equal-quantity further out-of-the-money long on the same expiry. The marketed book is never naked.

Bitcoin options persistently price implied volatility above the realised move that follows. Sellers of that insurance collect the spread. Short-dated paper pays theta quickly and carries overnight gamma. Defined Theta takes the theta, spends a small debit on the wing, and keeps strike-proximity and book-level equity stops on the ticket so one path cannot run to a margin event. Most tickets simply expire. In this sample, 180 of 182 did. Expiry is the scheduled close; it includes winning tickets and most of the 18 losers.

On a $100,000 account from 18 Aug 2025 to 22 Aug 2026 the book delivered +50.2% total return ($50,156; CAGR +49.4%) with a maximum intraday drawdown of −6.57% ($8,716). It was profitable on 90.1% of 182 tickets and in 11 of 13 months. Sharpe 4.02, Sortino 5.06, Calmar 7.51. Median ticket about $394. Worst loss −$6,253.

02 / Strategy Mechanics

Strategy Architecture and Execution Framework

On sessions when listed Bitcoin options look worth writing, Defined Theta inspects implied volatility against subsequent realised and reads how the skew is pricing the two wings. The job is to sell short-dated insurance that already looks expensive. Sessions that do not pay are skipped. Idle days are allowed. When the screen is quiet the book stays flat.

A ticket is a short listed option into the next daily expiry, sized to account equity, plus an equal-quantity further out-of-the-money long on the same expiry. The long is attached at the same moment as the short. The marketed book is never naked. The wing spends some of the credit collected on the short. It is a defined-risk constraint for the allocator, not a claim that losses disappear or that a gap cannot print a large red ticket.

After the open the position is marked until the next daily expiry. There is no mid-life rehedge and no discretionary overlay. The short remains the income leg. A strike-proximity stop and a book-level equity stop sit on the ticket from the open. In this sample the median hold was 16 hours: overnight, into the next daily expiry.

The intended close is expiry. Here 180 of 182 tickets expired; two proximity stops fired. Expiry includes winning tickets and most of the 18 losers. Typical winners are a few hundred dollars of collected premium (median $394). The 18 losers are larger, and the worst ticket still lost $6,253. That is the cycle: look, write or skip, hold overnight, expire or stop, recycle the next session.

05 / Why the Edge Persists

Structural Advantages and the Source of Alpha

Bitcoin listed options persistently print implied volatility above the realised move that follows. That gap is an insurance bid: hedgers pay up for protection, and the seller keeps the overpay when subsequent realised stays inside the premium. Defined Theta harvests that overnight, on short-dated paper, and only when implied already looks rich versus realised and listed skew is paying to write. One year of Deribit chain replay is long enough to see the pattern survive both quiet weeks and violent ones. The 90.1% ticket hit rate is the book working as designed: many small credits, a few large losers. Wings and stops bound the path. They do not create the premium. If implied stopped sitting above realised, or if the insurance bid on skew vanished, this strategy would have nothing left to sell.

1. Selective, not spray

The vol-risk premium is the raw material. The book does not write every session. It prefers implied that already looks rich versus realised, so the credit at entry is the expensive print, not the average print. Idle days are a feature of that filter.

2. Listed skew as a bid

Hedgers bid one wing of the listed curve; the other cheapens. Defined Theta reads that skew and writes where the insurance bid is paying. The exact rule is internal. For an allocator the point is that entry is not a coin-flip two-sided sale. It is a skew-aware sale of short-dated premium.

3. Overnight theta, bounded by expiry

Short-dated paper decays fast. Median hold in this sample was 16 hours, into the next daily expiry. That is the recycle: collect theta, start again. The cost of that speed is overnight gamma, which the wing and the stops are there to contain, not to deny.

4. Always hedged as a principle

Every short is opened with an equal-quantity further-OTM long on the same expiry. The wing attached on 182 of 182 opens. It caps the blow-up on a ticket that goes through the short strike and it spends some of the credit. It is not sold here as a PnL edge or as a device that makes short gamma safe.

5. Stops that rarely need to work

A strike-proximity stop and a book-level equity stop sit on the ticket from the open. They fired twice in 182 trades; 180 tickets expired. Intended mode: expire, do not hero-trade the loser. The worst ticket still lost $6,253. The stops bound the path. They do not make overnight gamma safe.

06 / Risk Considerations

Risk Factors and Mitigation Framework

This is a short-gamma overnight book. High ticket win rate is the design, not a promise that the next gap will be small. The numbers below are the ones that matter if the sample is a fair draw of Bitcoin's listed options market. They are also the ones that break if it is not.

⚠ Material Risk Considerations

  • Overnight gamma: Positions are held into the next daily expiry. A gap through the short strike can still print a large loss. The worst ticket in sample lost $6,253 on a $100,000 account.
  • Wings do not save the book: The further-OTM long is always on (182/182). It is a defined-risk constraint. It does not turn short gamma into a hedged coupon and it does not prevent the two proximity stops or the 18 losing tickets.
  • Sample length: 370 calendar days (18 Aug 2025 – 22 Aug 2026), 182 tickets. One year of Deribit history is not a full cycle of Bitcoin vol regimes. A quieter or more violent year can move Sharpe, drawdown, and the loser tail.
  • Fees and liquidity: Deribit fees in this sample were $9,825. At larger size, bid-ask and impact on short-dated listed options can eat more of the credit than the replay models.
  • Concentration: One venue (Deribit), one underlier (BTC), short-dated listed options. Exchange, collateral, and weekend-gap risk sit outside the P&L path.
