01 / Executive Summary

Premium Selling with Situational Short-Vol Selection

Hold7 DTE9 Short-Vol Dual (eq333, SL 2×) (Hold7 DTE9 Short-Vol Dual (eq333, SL 2×)) is a short-volatility strategy on Bitcoin that sells either a short call or a short put on each eligible trading day, depending on which direction the eq333 (dual) dual-ensemble signal identifies as having the stronger edge. The strategy targets out-of-the-money options with approximately nine days to expiry, held for a fixed seven-day window. Premium is collected at entry; positions are closed at the planned hold date or earlier if the 2× stop-loss triggers (Exit when buyback cost ≥ premium × (1 + 2) — i.e. max loss capped at 2× collected premium (sl20).).

This variant was selected as rank #2 in the Greenfield v8 short-vol search using the composite_v8 score across all seven equal calendar market segments (linear weights 1…7). Unlike IS/OOS hold-out protocols, v8 ranks candidates on the full backtest window — there is no blind segment withheld from selection. Over the period from October 2024 through 2026-06-01, the strategy generated a +126.08% net return on $100,000 initial capital, equivalent to a CAGR of +66.85%. Risk-adjusted metrics include a Sharpe ratio of 11.22, Sortino ratio of 5.21, and a maximum drawdown of 1.39% ($1,827 absolute). The strategy completed 352 trades with a 96.0% win rate and a profit factor of 14.5.

02 / Strategy Mechanics

How The Strategy Works

The strategy operates on a disciplined daily process. The eq333 (dual) dual ensemble evaluates the prevailing market environment and determines whether a short call or a short put offers the better risk/reward for that session. When the filter confirms an opportunity above threshold τ, the strategy selects an appropriate listed BTC option, sells at the prevailing bid (short entry), and holds until the planned exit or stop-loss. There is no discretionary override once a signal is confirmed.

05 / Sources of Edge

Why The Strategy Works

1. Short-Vol Theta Harvest

By selling option premium on short-dated contracts and holding for seven days, the strategy systematically collects time decay when the underlying does not move sharply against the position.

2. Situational Selection — Not a Fixed Bias

By selecting short calls or short puts based on the eq333 (dual) signal rather than maintaining a permanent directional view, the strategy adapts to both rising and falling BTC regimes within a single short-vol framework.

3. Phase Consistency Across Seven Segments

Positive net P&L was achieved in 7 of 7 market phases in this backtest (reference v8 search: 7/7 positive segments at selection). Consistent performance across calendar phases supports the composite_v8 ranking score.

4. Stop-Loss Discipline

The 2× premium stop-loss (sl20) closes positions before open-ended short-option losses can accumulate. Worst single trade: −$2,079.

5. Controlled Drawdown Profile

Maximum drawdown of 1.39% ($1,827) relative to total return demonstrates that capital growth was not driven by unchecked tail exposure.

06 / Risk Considerations

Important Risk Factors

⚠ Material Limitations

  • Backtest results are hypothetical. Past performance is not indicative of future results. Live trading may differ due to liquidity, slippage, and changing market structure.
  • Short-option tail risk. Selling naked options exposes the strategy to theoretically unlimited loss on sharp underlying moves. The stop-loss mitigates but cannot eliminate gap risk or execution slippage beyond the modelled cap.
  • Stop-loss limits loss per trade, not portfolio risk. Multiple concurrent or sequential stop-outs during volatile regimes can still produce meaningful drawdowns (1.39% observed).
  • Full-period v8 selection — no blind hold-out. Rank #2 was chosen using composite_v8 across all seven segments including the most recent phase. This improves coverage of recent regimes but does not provide an unseen forward-validation segment; overfitting to the full window remains possible.
  • Exceptional historical metrics. The win rate (96.0%) and total return (+126.08%) observed in backtesting are strong and may not persist at the same level going forward.
  • Implied volatility sensitivity. Short-vol strategies benefit from stable or falling implied vol. Sudden vol spikes increase buyback costs and can trigger stop-losses more frequently.
  • Bitcoin-specific risks. The strategy is concentrated in a single underlying asset class. Crypto market structure, exchange availability, and regulatory developments can affect execution and performance.
  • Liquidity and capacity. At moderate scale, Deribit BTC options provide sufficient depth. At significantly larger allocation sizes, entry and exit prices may be affected by market impact.
